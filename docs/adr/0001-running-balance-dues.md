# Due is a running balance, not month-slot allocation

Members' payments are recorded as plain money-in entries with a Credit Date (when the amount hit the bank); they are never allocated to specific months. A member's Due is one number: total billed (shares × price × months from joining to exit) minus total contributed. Overpayment becomes an Advance that carries forward.

Month-by-month status (the dashboard grid and member timeline) is derived for display by walking a member's entries against their monthly bills — it is a projection, not stored data. The walk allocates each Contribution to the oldest uncovered bill (true FIFO), regardless of the Contribution's own Credit Date; money left after every projected bill is the Advance, and months past the current one are rendered as upcoming projections at today's shares and price. Waivers and opening dues are handled as Adjustment entries rather than a month-allocation schema. Chosen because the team records money when it lands in the bank, so forcing an admin to pick which month a payment is "for" would be invented bookkeeping; the cost is that per-month history is recomputed rather than stored.
